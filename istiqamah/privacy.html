Privacy Policy

Effective 21 October 2026 · Applies to Istiqamah for Android (com.khanlabsltd.istiqamah)

At a glance

  • Your prayer record stays on your phone. We never receive it.
  • Prayer times and the Qibla direction are calculated on your phone.
  • No account, no sign-up, no ads, no analytics, no tracking.
  • We don't collect, sell or share your personal information.

This policy explains how Istiqamah ("the app"), a daily prayer tracker, handles your information. The app is made by Khan Labs Ltd ("we", "us"), an independent developer based in Pakistan.

Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. The app has no account system, no analytics, no advertising and no servers of our own, and it does not send your data over the internet. We cannot see your prayer record or anything else you do in the app.

What the app keeps on your phone

To work, the app saves the following only on your device:

  • which prayers you marked as prayed, by date, and your streaks
  • make-up (qada) prayer counts, if you use that feature
  • your settings: the location you chose, calculation method, minute adjustments, reminders, Hijri date adjustment and theme

Your practice is personal. This information is never sent to us.

Location

The app needs a location to calculate prayer times and the Qibla direction. You can choose a city from a list, or let the app use your phone's location.

If you choose "Use my location", the app asks for approximate location only, never precise location. Your coordinates are saved on your phone and used there for these calculations. The app does not track your location in the background and does not send it anywhere.

On Android, approximate location is provided by your phone's location services, which use Google Play services. See Google services below.

Compass

The Qibla screen reads your phone's compass and motion sensors while it is open, to point the way. Sensor readings are used on the spot and are not saved.

Prayer reminders

If you turn on reminders, the app schedules notifications on your phone for each prayer time. They are created and shown by your phone. No reminder information is sent to us or to a notification service.

Home-screen widget

If you add the Istiqamah widget, it shows your prayer times and today's progress on your home screen, using the same information saved on your phone. Anyone who can see your home screen can see what the widget shows.

Backups

Backup files you save. "Save backup" creates a file with your prayer record and make-up prayer counts, and opens Android's share menu so you can save it where you choose, for example Google Drive. "Restore from backup" lets you pick that file again. The app never uploads a backup by itself. Once you save the file somewhere, that copy is handled by the app or service you chose, under its own privacy policy.

Android's automatic backup. If backup is turned on in your phone's settings, Android may include the app's saved information in your own Google account backup, so it can be restored on a new phone. This backup is run by Android and Google, not by us, and we cannot access it. You can turn it off in your phone's settings.

Google services

The app uses Android and Google Play services for approximate location and, if you choose, automatic backup. Google may collect limited information through these services, for example to provide location, under the Google Privacy Policy. We do not receive any of that information.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your information stays on your phone until you change or delete it. You can unmark prayers and reset make-up prayer counts in the app at any time. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage in Android settings removes everything the app saved on your phone. Backup files you saved yourself are kept until you delete them. Because we never receive your data, we have nothing to delete on our side.

Children

The app is a general tool for adults and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If a future version handles data differently, we will update this policy and the app's Google Play listing before that version is released. The effective date at the top shows when the policy last changed.
